PUBLIC ADVISORY

The National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) wishes to inform the public that on 14 July 2026, it became aware of an online post alleging the unauthorized disclosure of files purportedly associated with the NTC-EDGE System.

Upon discovery of the incident, the Commission immediately activated its incident response protocols and commenced coordination with the Cybercrime Investigation and Coordinating Center (CICC), the third-party service provider administering for the EDGE System, and other concerned offices to validate the authenticity, scope, and extent of the alleged data breach. A technical investigation and forensic assessment remain ongoing.

Based on the Commission's preliminary examination, the allegedly disclosed files may contain various categories of personal information associated with regulatory applications and supporting documents submitted to the Commission. The precise number of affected data subjects and the full extent of the information involved, remain subject to verification.

As a precautionary measure, individuals who may have transacted with the NTC through the NTC-EDGE System are advised to:
- Remain vigilant against phishing emails, fraudulent text messages, phone calls, or social engineering attempts.
- Do not disclose passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), PINs, or other confidential credentials in response to unsolicited communications.
- Monitor financial and online accounts for any suspicious or unauthorized activity.
- Immediately report any suspected identity theft, fraud, or misuse of personal information to the appropriate authorities and financial institutions, where applicable.

The Commission is implementing appropriate technical and administrative measures to address the incident, including continued forensic analysis, validation of the allegedly compromised data, coordination with relevant government agencies, and strengthening of existing security controls to prevent similar incidents.

The NTC recognizes the importance of protecting the personal data entrusted to it and remains committed to transparency and compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 and applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission.

Further updates will be issued as additional verified information becomes available.
