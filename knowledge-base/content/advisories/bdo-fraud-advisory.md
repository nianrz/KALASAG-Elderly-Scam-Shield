#BDOStopScam

Know the ways you can protect yourself against scams. Be smarter than a scammer with #BDOStopScam.

Together, we can outsmart scammers

We're committed to helping you become more equipped at identifying the scams that target your hard-earned money. Learn how to proactively protect your personal information and prevent financial loss with #BDOStopScam.

Beware of these common scams

Scam emails (Phishing)
Don't click links! Double-check your "BDO" emails and make sure they're from official senders and email addresses.

Scam calls (Vishing)
Don't share your OTP! Anyone asking for sensitive account or card info, or your OTP is out to scam you. End the call right away.

Scam texts (Smishing)
Don't click links! BDO messages will not contain links, and sender IDs can be spoofed. Watch out for fake texts!

How to stay protected from scams

Identify
Is the message triggering panic? Requires you to act fast and has a link? If you say yes to all these, it's a scam.

Avoid
Take extra measures to protect your personal info to avoid getting scammed. Don't click links and never share your OTP.

Prevent
In case of a fraud experience, reset your online banking password and lock your card on BDO Online. Call us at (02) 8888-0000.

How to reset your online banking password on BDO Online:
Step 1: On the login page, tap "Need help logging in?", then "I'd like to reset my password."
Step 2: Enter your username and the last four (4) digits of your account or card number, then select Next.
Step 3: Enter the OTP sent to your registered mobile number, then select Submit.
Step 4: Set up your new password. Once successful, you can already use your new password to log in to the BDO Online app and website.

How to lock your Debit or Credit Card on BDO Online:
Step 1: Once you're logged in, select More, then Manage Cards.
Step 2: Choose the Debit or Credit Card that you would like to lock, then toggle on Lock Card.
Step 3: You will get notified when the Debit or Credit Card is successfully locked.
